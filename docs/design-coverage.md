# Orbit Plus — Design Requirement Coverage

## Participant Coverage

| Screens | Requirements |
|---|---|
| REPORT-01 through REPORT-05 | US-01 through US-06; FR-01 through FR-15 |

The participant screens represent registration, discovery, event details, registration review, dashboard, status management, profile, and notifications.

## Organizer Coverage

| Screens | Requirements |
|---|---|
| REPORT-06 and REPORT-07 | US-07 through US-10; FR-16 through FR-19 |

The organizer screens represent event creation, event management, registered participants, export, registration-status management, and participant communication.

## Volunteer / Staff Coverage

| Screens | Requirements |
|---|---|
| REPORT-08 | US-11 through US-13; FR-20 through FR-23 |

## Sponsor Coverage

| Screens | Requirements |
|---|---|
| REPORT-09 and REPORT-10 | US-14 through US-16; FR-24 through FR-27 |

The sponsor screens represent sponsorship submission and completed sponsored-event analytics.

## Admin Coverage

| Screens | Requirements |
|---|---|
| REPORT-11 and REPORT-12 | US-17 through US-20; FR-28 through FR-32 |

The admin screens represent account approvals, user management, event monitoring, and moderation-related visibility.

## Coverage Principle

A requirement is considered visually represented when the relevant action, information, status, or role permission is understandable from the Figma screen.

The design is not treated as production implementation or proof that backend functionality has been completed.
